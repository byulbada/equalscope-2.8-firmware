Features

What it does

Live pressure graph

Watch your mouth pressure in real time on a 2.8" touch screen. Pinch to zoom, swipe to scroll.

Reference overlay

Save a good session as a reference and see it overlaid on your live graph. Your best run becomes the target.

Every session saved

Sessions are stored automatically as CSV — on internal memory or a microSD card. Open them in Excel or Google Sheets and analyse them any way you like.

EQ interval trainer

Build an alarm sequence — "every 2 s × 5, then every 4 s × 5" — to rehearse the equalization rhythm of a descent. Alarm points are marked on the graph so you can compare plan against reality.

Calibrated pressure valve

A numbered dial lets you repeat the exact same resistance every time — simulating the shrinking air volume of depth on dry land (especially useful for mouthfill).

English built in

The device menu is available in English, and firmware updates are installed straight from an SD card — no PC software required.

Pattern analysis

It recognises how you equalize

From the shape of the pressure curve, Equalscope estimates which of six equalization patterns your session resembles. It reads pressure, not anatomy — treat the result as a reference, not a diagnosis.

ValsalvaSlow build from lungs and breathing muscles, held at the peak
Valsalva + FrenzelMixed — a shoulder on the way up, then a sharp push
FrenzelShort, sharp pulses from air in the mouth
MouthfillA long, flat hold on mouth air
Mouthfill FrenzelNarrow pulses riding on top of held mouth air
BTV (hands-free)Opening the tubes without pushing — very low pressure oscillation
