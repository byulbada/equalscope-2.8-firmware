Safety

Out of the water only — you can black out without warning. No hyperventilating before a hold, stop at once if you feel unwell. In water, always with a buddy or instructor.

Care

Do not leave it in the car. A car in summer reaches 60–70 °C. Left hot for long, the battery can swell or catch fire, and the case warps.
